# Finkkle

**Technology that works around you, not the other way round.**

Most software still expects you to do the adapting. You learn the menus, remember the commands, and repeat yourself until it gets the message. We think that's backwards.

At Finkkle, we build tools that pick up on context, stay out of your way, and actually help you get things done.

---

## Igno

Igno is where most of our energy is going right now. It's a personal AI assistant for everyday life, built mobile-first.

We're not trying to make another chat window. We want Igno to be the thing you reach for when you need to think something through, write something, keep track of something, or just get it off your plate. Conversation, useful tools, and the ability to take action, all in one place.

It's still early, and it'll keep changing as we learn what people actually need from it.

---

## What we care about

- **Useful first.** If it doesn't solve a real problem, it doesn't ship, however good the demo looks.
- **Simple on the surface.** Powerful shouldn't have to mean complicated.
- **AI where it earns its place.** Not sprinkled on everything just because we can.
- **Your data, your call.** You should always know what's happening with your information, and stay in control of it.
- **Built properly.** Reliable, fast, and easy to maintain. We'd rather do it right than do it twice.
- **Never finished.** We ship, listen, and keep improving.
